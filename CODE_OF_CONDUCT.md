# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this repository to be welcoming, respectful, and useful for everyone.

By participating, you agree to:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus on ideas, code, and outcomes—not personal attacks.
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions.
- Provide feedback that is specific and actionable.
- Respect privacy and never post secrets or sensitive data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, hate speech, or intimidation.
- Personal insults, trolling, or repeated bad-faith disruption.
- Sharing private/confidential information without permission.
- Spam, off-topic promotion, or intentionally harmful guidance.

## Scope

This policy applies in this repository’s issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project communication channels.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this policy, report it privately to the maintainers.

- Preferred: open a private maintainers-only channel where available (for example, a private discussion or direct maintainer contact).
- Security issues: follow `SECURITY.md` instead of public issue threads.

Maintainers will review reports promptly and may take action such as warning, moderation, content removal, or access restrictions.

## Maintainer standards

Maintainers are expected to model respectful behavior, apply rules consistently, and prioritize community safety.
